---
description: Collect a status snapshot across the team
---
Give a **standup snapshot** for the current feature:

- Each active story/task: owner + status (**done / in-progress / blocked**), one line each.
- Blockers, and exactly what's needed to clear each.
- The next 1–3 actions and who owns them.

Keep it tight — a scannable status board, not a narrative.
